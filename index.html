Language Practice (Browser Only)

Listen, speak, and get instant feedback without servers or paid APIs.

How this works

  • Everything runs in your browser using the Web Speech API.
  • No servers, no paid APIs, and lessons come from static JSON files.
  • Hover or tap words to see translations in your base language.
  • Your language choices and progress are stored locally in your browser.

Add new lessons by dropping JSON files into /data. The filename should follow {lang}_a1_introductions.json style and include lang, level, theme, and sentences fields.
